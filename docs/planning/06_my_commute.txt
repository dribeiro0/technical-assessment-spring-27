ENROUTE - 06 MY COMMUTE
Planning baseline 0.1 - November 24, 2026
Related: 04, 05, 03 (commute scope), 09 (privacy, accessibility), 11

My Commute is a high-priority product capability [ACCEPTED]. In the
assessment it is an Extra Feature ("saved commutes"), not a core
requirement (10).

======================================================================
1. WHAT IT ANSWERS                                         [ACCEPTED]
======================================================================

  1. When should I leave for the station?
  2. When will my train arrive?
  3. When should I get off?


  Direction [ACCEPTED]: monitor journeys the rider already knows. Full
  route planning between any two places is not in scope now.

======================================================================
2. LEVELS OF PERSONALIZATION                               [ACCEPTED]
======================================================================

  Saved stop       upcoming departures at one stop, chosen direction
                   e.g. BU Central, eastbound
  Saved journey    conditions between two stops on a route
                   e.g. BU Central -> Park Street (Green B)
  Active journey   following one specific vehicle now

  A rider must not have to build a full commute just to see the next
  train.

  A saved journey holds: name, origin stop, optional destination,
  route/branch, direction, optional walking time, optional buffer,
  optional accessibility preference (step-free). Riders can save
  several, rename, reverse direction, and delete them. No account.

======================================================================
3. THE JOURNEY, STEP BY STEP
======================================================================

  [1] SAVE
      pick station -> pick route/branch -> pick direction -> optional
      destination -> optional walk time and buffer -> name it
      No location permission needed: station search works by name.

  [2] BEFORE LEAVING
      sees: next departures (LIVE / SCHEDULED labels), DIRECT/INDIRECT
      alerts, commute Chaos Score (if shipped, with confidence), and a
      departure recommendation:

         T_leave = T_arrival - T_walk - T_buffer

      Example [MOCK]: train in 15 min, 6-min walk, 3-min buffer
                      -> "Leave in 6 minutes"
      If T_leave < 0: "You may miss this one" and show the next.
      Uses the official prediction. If only a scheduled time exists,
      the recommendation says it is based on the schedule.

  [3] WAITING
      tracking view: countdown, where the train is, stops away,
      destination/headsign, relevant alerts, next departure after it.

  [4] BOARDING
      rider taps "I'm on this train". Explicit confirmation is the
      baseline. [ACCEPTED: automatic boarding detection is later]

  [5] ONBOARD
      next stop, stops remaining, estimated arrival at destination,
      disruptions ahead on the remaining segment.

  [6] APPROACHING DESTINATION
      "Your stop is next - get off at Park Street", shown in-app when
      the vehicle's stop progress is reliable. Optional sound and
      vibration where the browser allows (09). If progress is not
      reliable, say so rather than guess.

  [7] END
      arrives at destination, or rider taps "End tracking" at any time.
      Tracking stops immediately. Optional local record of the trip.

  Lifecycle names: Planning -> Waiting -> Boarding ->
  Onboard -> (Transferring) -> Completed. Transfers are architecturally
  allowed for later; single-route journeys come first.

======================================================================
4. FAILURE AND EDGE CASES
======================================================================

  Situation                            Behaviour
  -----------------------------------  ---------------------------------
  Train cannot be identified           show departures without "track";
  (no vehicle/trip match)              say "live tracking not available
                                       for this departure"
  Rider boards a different train       let them pick the vehicle manually
  Train changes destination / short-   warn; if destination no longer on
  turns                                remaining stops, say so and offer
                                       next suitable departure
  Destination stop skipped or closed   show the relevant alert; warn
                                       before the previous stop
  Rider passes destination             warn only when reliably detected
  Data goes stale                      freeze countdown, show "last
                                       updated N min ago", no get-off
                                       alert based on stale data
  Vehicle disappears                   "tracking uncertain", keep last
                                       known stop and time
  Prediction changes                   recalculate countdown and T_leave
  Missed train                         offer next departure
  Connection drops                     keep saved journeys; show last
                                       known information with its age
  Notification permission denied       in-app alert still works; explain
                                       that background alerts are off
  Location permission denied           everything works via search
  Rider ends tracking                  stop all tracking updates and
                                       alerts for that journey at once
  No saved commutes                    nearby (if allowed) or search,
                                       plus a short onboarding

======================================================================
5. COMMUTE-SPECIFIC SCORING
======================================================================

  Commute Chaos uses only the journey's boarding stop, destination,
  direction, time window, segments, and (later) transfers (03).

  Accessibility-aware scoring [PROPOSED]: the same elevator outage can
  matter a lot to a rider who needs step-free access and not at all to
  another. Example [MOCK]: Passenger A 12/100, Passenger B (needs
  step-free) 90/100. Applies only where facility data supports it.

======================================================================
7. ACCEPTANCE CRITERIA
======================================================================

  - With location permission denied, a rider can save a stop and see
    its departures.
  - Saved journeys survive a page reload on the same device without an
    account.
  - A departure without a live prediction is labelled "Scheduled" and so
    is any recommendation based on it.
  - "Your stop is next" appears in-app before the destination when the
    vehicle's stop progress is fresh; it does not appear when data is
    stale.
  - Tapping "End tracking" stops updates and alerts for that journey.
  - With notifications denied, the in-app alert still appears.

  Built in Sprint 4 (November 27-28, 2026; 10 section 10):
  - Saved from a station card; opened from the sidebar as a card on the map
    (DEC-047). Stop, direction, optional destination, usual walk and buffer;
    several per device; edit, reverse and delete in the card.
  - Walk time from the rider's location when they turn it on (an estimate,
    never stored or sent; DEC-048), otherwise the usual walk they typed.
  - Departures labelled LIVE or SCHEDULED, with a leave-time recommendation
    that says when it is based on the timetable.
  - Not yet: alerts for the journey (Sprint 5), Commute Chaos Score
    (Sprint 7), waiting, onboard and get-off tracking (Sprint 8, future
    expansion, DEC-063).
