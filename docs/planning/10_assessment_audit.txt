9. AUDIT OF NOVEMBER 25, 2026
======================================================================

  How it was checked
  - Every check in section 1 was run against the real app and the live MBTA feed.
  - Error states were triggered on purpose. A test build was pointed at a local
    stand-in for the MBTA API that could fail (HTTP 500) or rate-limit
    (HTTP 429) on a timed plan. A real MBTA 429 was also observed.
  - On-screen counts were compared with the exact /vehicles response the
    page received.
  - Colour contrast was computed for every text and marker pair.
  - An independent code review ran in parallel.

  Passed without changes
  - First-load failure shows an error panel with Retry, never a blank page.
  - A failed refresh keeps the last trains and their time; stale after 45 s.
  - A rate limit shows a countdown and sends no requests until it ends.
  - Map, status bar, chart and table counts match the API exactly.
  - Visible keyboard focus; state never shown by colour alone.
  - Single page with one document load; title and description on all sizes.

  Found and fixed (commit 6fff8bc)
  - A long pause or outage aged every train out and emptied the map,
    which looked like "no trains". Ages are now judged against the last
    successful fetch (DEC-037).
  - Enter on a focused marker opened its popup but did not select the
    train. The card now opens from the keyboard too.
  - Line paths and stations never retried after a failed first load; they
    now retry every minute.
  - Marker screen-reader titles kept the first stop label; they now update.
  - Orange markers were 2.94:1 on the map (3:1 needed); now 3.23:1.
  - Only the request that hit a 429 waited; all requests now share one wait
    (DEC-036).
  - Changing the line filter kept a train selected on another line.
  - No refresh on returning to the tab.
  - Counts showed 0 before any data; they now show "–". The status bar says
    "rate limited" on a first-load 429.
  - Countdowns rounded down and passed stops lingered.
  - Map zoom still animated under reduced motion.

  Open (minor, not blocking)
  - The phone drawer does not trap focus or return it on close.
  - Closing a popup keeps the train selected.
  - Direction falls back to "Direction unavailable" when a trip has no headsign.
  - The "paused more than 10 minutes" fix was checked by reading the code,
    not by waiting 10 minutes.

10. SPRINT 4 AUDIT (MY COMMUTE), NOVEMBER 27-28, 2026
======================================================================

  Sprint 4 is an optional extra ("saved commutes"); R1-R10 do not depend
  on it. Acceptance criteria C1-C10 were agreed before building.

  How it was checked
  - 26 new unit tests (69 in total), type check, lint and format.
  - A headless browser ran each flow against the real MBTA feed, and
    against the local stand-in API extended with the commute requests
    (empty live predictions, HTTP 500 from the start, healthy then failing).
  - Requests were counted over 62 s; contrast was computed for every new
    colour pair.

  Results
  C1  Save without location: station by name, line, direction, optional
      destination, walk, buffer, name. Saved on the real feed. VERIFIED
  C2  Three commutes stored beforehand appeared after a fresh load. VERIFIED
  C3  Rename, reverse (stations swap, direction flips) and delete with an
      on-page "Keep it" confirmation. VERIFIED
  C4  Live rows labelled LIVE; with no live predictions the rows, advice and
      footer say SCHEDULED / "based on the timetable". VERIFIED
  C5  15 min train, 6 walk, 3 buffer gives "Leave in 6 min" (unit test);
      missed trains are counted ("You may miss the next 2 trains"). VERIFIED
  C6  First-load failure explains itself and says the commute is still
      saved; after 45 s without fresh data the countdown freezes to clock
      times, advice is withheld and the failed refresh is named. VERIFIED
  C7  62 s with three saved commutes: 17 requests including 5 one-time
      loads; steady state 9 per minute (vehicles 4, alerts 1, the open
      commute 4). Closed commutes and the timetable cost nothing while
      live data exists. VERIFIED
  C8  390 px phone with a saved commute opened / with its card as the
      bottom sheet (/?commute=...); desktop stayed on the map. VERIFIED
      (re-run after the redesign below)
  C9  Text contrast 4.82:1 or more; input borders raised from 1.93:1 to
      5.95:1; each departure row reads as one sentence to screen readers;
      native radios and selects keep keyboard support. VERIFIED
  C10 All earlier tests pass; one document load across map, Analytics, My
      commute and back; map, lines and chart unchanged. VERIFIED

  Found and fixed during the audit
  - Saving an edit while the destination list had not loaded dropped the
    destination. The saved destination, station and line now stay as
    choices.
  - "Aim for the one after" was wrong when two trains were out of reach.
  - The leave-time box was a live region and would have been announced
    every minute.
  - The ribbon had no My commute link.

  Open (minor)
  - A countdown can read "9 min" while 9 minutes of walk and buffer marks
    that train as missed: the countdown rounds, the advice rounds down.
  - The station picker is a native list with type-to-jump, not a search box.
  - Timetable fallback was tested with recorded data, not during a real
    early-morning gap.

  Redesign, November 28 (DEC-047, DEC-048)
  - Review: the separate tab felt unnatural, and a typed walk time is only
    right from one place. My Commute became a card on the map, saved from
    the station card, and the walk can come from the rider's location.
  - Checked in the browser on the real feed: station card -> "Save as my
    commute" -> form in the card with the station chosen -> saved commute
    card (?commute=), listed in the sidebar; no page errors. With location
    refused, the card says so and uses the usual walk.
  - 4 new unit tests for the walking estimate (73 in total).
  - Not checked in a browser: a granted location (the test browser cannot
    grant it). The estimate itself is unit tested; try it on a phone.
  - Open: on a phone the ringed station can sit under the bottom sheet.

  Commute focus on the map, November 28 (DEC-049, DEC-050)
  - Stretch cutting checked against the real MBTA network for eight
    commutes, including both Red branches, a Green branch with no
    destination, Orange and Blue. The first version wrongly put Savin Hill
    on the Braintree branch (parallel tracks); fixed with the branch table
    and a regression test.
  - In the browser (desktop and 390 px phone): opening the commute from the
    sidebar zooms to the stretch beside or above the card, fades the rest,
    and shows only Braintree-bound trains for Kendall -> Quincy Center.
  - Found while checking: the advice aimed a Quincy Center rider at an
    Ashmont train. Departures now count only trains that reach the
    destination; the card says "toward Braintree".
  - 10 new unit tests (83 in total).
  - Short trips are now excluded too (DEC-051); see the re-audit below.

  Re-audit before merging Sprint 4, November 28
  - C6 re-run on the commute card with the local stand-in API: timetable
    fallback (SCHEDULED, one timetable request only when live was empty),
    stale data after 45 s (clock times, no advice, failure named) and a
    first-load failure (explained, commute kept). No page errors.
  - C7 re-run on the real feed with three saved commutes and one card open:
    17 requests in 62 s, including 5 one-time loads; steady state 9 a
    minute; the closed commutes and the timetable cost nothing.
  - Short trips: 8 cases on the real network (Red both ways, Green-C,
    Orange) all correct; 4 new unit tests (87 in total).
  - Fixed while checking: the out-of-date banner was hidden under the card
    on desktop (now top-left, DEC-052); the card no longer shows a data
    source when nothing has loaded; the station card says "Loading station…"
    instead of "Station" while names load.
  - Open (minor): at some window sizes the card overlaps a corner of the
    first-load error panel; its message and Retry button stay visible.
